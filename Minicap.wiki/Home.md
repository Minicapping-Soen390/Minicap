# Welcome to the MiniCappin Team Wiki for SOEN 390

This wiki is the official documentation repository for Team MiniCappin's SOEN 390 project, maintained by the **Scrum Master**. It serves as our single source of truth for key project artifacts, practices, and progress tracking.

## 🎯 Source of Truth Policy

**This Wiki is our SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH**. In cases of conflicting information:
- The wiki's content takes precedence over verbal agreements
- All decisions must be documented here
- External documents should reference wiki pages
- If it's not in the wiki, it's not official

*   **Testing Reports (Per Release):**  Comprehensive reports detailing testing activities and results for each project release.
*   **Templates:** Reusable templates for various project documents and processes.

**Contributing to the Wiki:**

While direct editing is managed by the Scrum Master, **all team members are encouraged to contribute!**  If you identify necessary updates, improvements, or find issues, please:

1.  **Create a GitHub Issue:** For small corrections, suggestions, or questions.
2.  **Submit a Markdown File (MD):** For more substantial content contributions or proposed rewrites.
3.  **Link External Documents:** When creating external reports, always reference the relevant wiki pages.

The Scrum Master will review all contributions and update the Wiki accordingly.

This Wiki is a vital resource for our team.  By keeping it current and accurate, we ensure everyone has access to the information needed for a successful project.
